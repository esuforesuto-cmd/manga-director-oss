# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
all contributors and users, regardless of experience, identity, or background.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and mindful of differing experiences. Focus
discussion on ideas and evidence. Do not publish private information, use
abusive language, or engage in harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or
retaliation.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, review,
and project-related community spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub
reporting mechanisms. Maintainers may remove content, issue warnings, or limit
participation to protect the community.
