# Long-Term Support Policy

## Official v6.x LTS line

`6.0.x` is the current long-term support line for manga-director. It maintains
the v6.0 Creative Production Platform while preserving v5.x compatibility and
the frozen Platform API v1.0, SDK v1.0, Extension API v1.0, and Marketplace
Specification v1.0.

## Support lifecycle

An LTS line is active from its official designation until an end-of-life notice
is published under [END_OF_LIFE_POLICY.md](END_OF_LIFE_POLICY.md). During that
period, maintainers accept compatible correctness, security, documentation,
and reliability fixes. New capabilities require a separate versioned roadmap
and are not added to the LTS line.

## Maintenance process

Every maintenance proposal follows the compatibility, backport, and security
policies. It must retain StateMachine authority and the one-Page production
invariants. Operational detail is maintained in [docs/V6_LTS_PLAN.md](docs/V6_LTS_PLAN.md).
