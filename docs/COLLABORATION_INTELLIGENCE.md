# Collaboration Intelligence

`V44EnterpriseIntelligenceService.collaboration_intelligence(project_id, context)`
analyzes v4.4 Team Foundation evidence into immutable collaboration metrics and
human-review insight DTOs.

The report observes declared member and review counts and identifies storyboard
and completed-quality-review prerequisites. It cannot change membership or
permissions, assign or dispatch work, send messages, complete a review, grant
approval, persist metrics, or change workflow state.
