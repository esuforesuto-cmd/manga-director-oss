# Intelligence Governance

v4.6 Iteration 3 adds `IntelligenceGovernanceReport`, an immutable policy,
compliance, and summary projection over the Intelligence Dashboard.

`V46IntelligenceGovernanceService.intelligence_governance()` retains
StateMachine authority, exactly-one-page scope, persisted-storyboard evidence,
completed-quality-review evidence, and human review requirements. It cannot
enforce or persist a policy, confirm compliance, make an autonomous decision,
approve a page, or change a workflow.
