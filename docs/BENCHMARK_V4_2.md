# v4.2.0 Benchmark Verification

Provider-free micro-benchmarks cover Autonomous Execution, Checkpoint,
Supervisor, Long-running Task, Goal Management, Adaptive Planning, Pipeline,
Recovery, Supervision, Governance, Monitoring, and Reliability DTO projections.
The final local smoke completed 1,000 constructions per target in
0.004425-0.007724 seconds.

No local performance regression requiring a corrective change was identified.
These figures are DTO-construction evidence only; they do not claim provider,
backend, worker, persistent queue, network, database, or SLO performance.
