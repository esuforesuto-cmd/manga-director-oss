# v2.6.0 Benchmark Verification

The stable release retains the RC1 provider-free benchmark smoke for workflow,
repository, planning, analysis, Provider/Backend runtime, automation,
diagnostics, reporting, and health. No local regression was identified against
the v2.5 provider-free baseline. Results are smoke evidence rather than a
cross-host production SLO; live providers and distributed execution are out of
scope.

See [the RC benchmark record](BENCHMARK_V2_6_RC1.md) for review-host timings.
