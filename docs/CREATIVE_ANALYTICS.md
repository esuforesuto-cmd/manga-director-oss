# Creative Analytics

v3.5 Iteration 2 adds Story Analytics, Character Analytics, Page Quality
Analysis, Creative Trend, Recommendation Summary, and Dashboard DTOs. They
interpret supplied, one-Page Foundation evidence for human review.

No report generates content, changes a story or storyboard, completes quality
review, grants approval, persists a trend, or applies a recommendation.

- CLI: `manga-director director creative-analytics-v35 --project <id>`
- FastAPI: `GET /v3.5/creative-analytics`
- MCP: `creative_analytics_v35`
