# v3.5 Platform Analytics Foundation

Platform Health, Analytics Dashboard, Trend Summary, Historical Analysis,
Executive KPI, Report, and Dashboard DTOs aggregate supplied local v3.5
foundation evidence. They are presentation-independent and usable by optional
CLI, FastAPI, and MCP delivery adapters.

The foundation cannot collect remotely, persist telemetry or trends, monitor,
enforce a target, authorize a release, or take an external action.
