# Knowledge Exchange

Knowledge Exchange defines a local planning record for sharing-readiness, not
a knowledge synchronization service. A record may describe a redacted knowledge
namespace, schema, classification, relationship trace, quality evidence,
provenance, owner, recipient, consent, compatibility, and rollback boundary.

The design prohibits remote search, transfer, persistence, synchronization,
merge, replication, ownership change, access enforcement, or automatic use of
knowledge. Knowledge Repository interfaces remain unchanged.

Human owners retain control of disclosure and future exchange participation.
