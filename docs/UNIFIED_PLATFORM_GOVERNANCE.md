# Unified Platform Governance

`UnifiedPlatformGovernanceService` composes a policy, compliance placeholder,
and human-review summary from an existing Unified Platform Dashboard. It makes
the StateMachine, one-Page, storyboard, quality-review, and human-approval
requirements visible without enforcing, persisting, approving, or changing
them.

Compliance remains unconfirmed until a human reviews the relevant owner
evidence. The report cannot grant permissions or replace any existing
Governance implementation.
