# Team Foundation

`V44EnterpriseFoundationService.team(project_id, context)` returns immutable
team, human-owner member, review prerequisite, and summary DTOs. Team records
are descriptive evidence for commercial review planning, not a collaboration
runtime.

The foundation cannot create a team, change membership or permissions, assign
or dispatch work, message a user, complete quality review, approve a Page, or
bypass storyboard and StateMachine requirements.
