# v3.2.0 Creative Studio Guide

Creative Studio combines existing Project, Page, planning, and review evidence
into typed workspace, session, layout, dashboard, activity, insight,
reliability, and readiness views. It is a human planning aid and has no write,
Agent-dispatch, transition, image-generation, quality-pass, or approval
authority.

Use the DTOs to inspect exactly one Page and its legal next step. Persisted
storyboard and completed-quality-review requirements remain solely enforced by
the existing StateMachine.
