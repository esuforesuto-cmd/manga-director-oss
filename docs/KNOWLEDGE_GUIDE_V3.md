# v3.0.0 Knowledge Guide

Knowledge Foundation and Creative Knowledge project existing `ProjectRepository`
data into immutable, redacted namespaces, indexes, snapshots, searches,
relationships, summaries, integrity, governance, quality, and risk reports.
The interfaces are additive: they do not create a new knowledge store or mutate
Repository data.

Use the reports as human-review evidence for planning and operations. Metadata
values, semantic retrieval, retention enforcement, automatic policy changes,
and autonomous workflow decisions remain intentionally out of scope.
