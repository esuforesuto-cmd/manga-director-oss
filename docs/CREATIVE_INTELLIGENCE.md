# Creative Intelligence Planning

Creative Dashboard, Creative Metrics, Story Analytics, Character Analytics,
Page Quality Metrics, and Creative Recommendations are candidate DTO reports.
They may summarize supplied planning and completed quality evidence for human
review only.

No candidate may generate content, rewrite a plan, modify a storyboard,
automatically choose a creative direction, bypass review, or approve a Page.
See [the v3.5 roadmap](ROADMAP_v3_5.md).
