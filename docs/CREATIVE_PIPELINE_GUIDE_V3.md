# v3.0.0 Creative Pipeline Guide

Creative planning and governance compose Story, Chapter, Page, and Panel DTOs
with policy, standards, compliance, and audit evidence. These reports make the
existing creative workflow visible without becoming an execution path.

They cannot generate an image, alter a persisted storyboard, skip a workflow
stage, pass quality, or approve a Page. The StateMachine remains the source of
truth for every legal transition and human approval remains required.
