# Quality Governance

`QualityGovernanceReport` exposes quality policy identifier, revision, human
review requirement, and StateMachine authority alongside the existing
release-readiness dashboard. It is governance evidence, not policy enforcement.

The report cannot modify policies, grant permissions, approve a Page, change a
workflow, or authorize a release. v5.0 LTS and v5.3 consumers remain optional
participants with no required configuration or migration.
