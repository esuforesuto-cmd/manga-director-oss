# Review Intelligence Design

## Purpose

Review Intelligence turns explicitly supplied review evidence into a coherent,
human-readable analysis. It aligns creative, editorial, quality, workflow,
governance, and operational findings without replacing an existing quality
review or issuing an approval.

## Proposed review flow

1. Receive caller-supplied, attributable, redacted review findings.
2. Describe coverage, consistency, missing evidence, uncertainty, and risk.
3. Link findings to decision alternatives and recommendation prerequisites.
4. Produce a reviewer-ready report and escalation recommendation.
5. Leave review completion, approval, persistence, and workflow transitions to
   existing authoritative services and humans.

## Boundaries

Review Intelligence cannot generate or change content, mark a review complete,
approve a page, bypass quality gates, alter a finding, execute a workflow,
collect telemetry, or notify an external user or service.
