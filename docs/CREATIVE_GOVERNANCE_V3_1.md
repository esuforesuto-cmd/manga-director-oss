# Creative Governance v3.1

Creative Governance v3.1 is a one-Page diagnostic projection of existing
storyboard, quality, and human-approval guard evidence. It provides policy,
validation, compliance, quality score, summary, and a governance report.

The score expresses visible evidence only. It does not pass quality, apply a
policy, remediate findings, approve a Page, or transition StateMachine state.

## Delivery

- CLI: `manga-director director creative-governance-v31 --project <id> --page <n>`
- FastAPI: `GET /v3.1/creative-governance`
- MCP: `creative_governance_v31`

See [the v3.1 example](../examples/creative_governance/run_v31.py).
