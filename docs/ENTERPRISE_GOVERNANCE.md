# Enterprise Governance

`V44EnterpriseGovernanceService.enterprise_governance(project_id, context)`
returns immutable Enterprise Policy, Workspace Compliance, and Governance
Summary DTOs over the Enterprise Dashboard evidence.

The policy makes StateMachine authority, one-Page scope, persisted-storyboard,
completed-quality-review, and human approval prerequisites visible. It cannot
persist or enforce policy, confirm compliance, alter workspace membership,
approve a page, or transition a workflow.
