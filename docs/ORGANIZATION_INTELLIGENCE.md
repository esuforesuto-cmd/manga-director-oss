# Organization Intelligence Planning

Organization Intelligence candidates model redacted, supplied evidence for team
health, role analytics, workload distribution, collaboration metrics,
organization risk, and delivery confidence. Their purpose is to aid transparent
human discussion, not to evaluate people or automate organizational decisions.

Each Issue requires a data-minimization and redaction model, attribution and
retention policy, fixture provenance, explainable assumptions, and a proof that
it cannot assign work, alter roles, change membership, or commit delivery.
