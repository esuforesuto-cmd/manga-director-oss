# Production Intelligence Planning for v3.5

Production Efficiency, Pipeline Analytics, Capacity Forecast, Risk Prediction,
Delivery Intelligence, and Operational Insights are local, supplied-evidence
analysis candidates. Forecasts must document assumptions, uncertainty, and
their non-authoritative meaning.

They cannot schedule, allocate, optimize a live pipeline, change a workflow,
remediate, deploy, or commit delivery. See [the v3.5 roadmap](ROADMAP_v3_5.md).
